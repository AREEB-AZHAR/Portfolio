A little context

Curious across the stack.
Serious about the craft.

My work doesn’t sit in one narrow lane. I enjoy moving from a data model to an interface, or from an algorithm to the product experience around it.

Over three years of my BS Software Engineering degree, I’ve built coursework, personal products, freelance ecommerce work, and an adaptive Unity degree project. That range taught me how to learn unfamiliar tools quickly and connect technical decisions to the people using the software.

I’m most energised by product engineering, front-end systems, intelligent applications, and teams where I can own a problem end to end. I value readable code, thoughtful details, and explaining technical trade-offs clearly.

01

Build the whole loop

Understand the problem, model the system, craft the interface, and verify the result.

02

Make complexity legible

Good software should make difficult systems feel understandable to both users and teammates.

03

Stay evidence-led

Prefer working prototypes, measured behaviour, and honest constraints over impressive-sounding claims.
